# Privacy

GE Trade Journal records Grand Exchange offer activity in local files under `~/.runelite/ge-trade-journal/` (or `%USERPROFILE%\.runelite\ge-trade-journal\` on Windows).

Records include character names, item IDs and names, GE slot and order details, filled quantities, GP amounts, timestamps, and locally generated identifiers.

The plugin does not transmit this data or make network requests. It does not access your password or place trades. You control the files stored on your computer; avoid sharing exports that include your character name.
